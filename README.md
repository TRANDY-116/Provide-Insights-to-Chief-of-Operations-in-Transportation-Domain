# GoodCabs Performance Analysis

Data analysis project examining **GoodCabs**, a tier-2 city cab service in India, to identify performance gaps and growth opportunities across 10 cities over 6 months.

## Business Context
As a Data Analyst for GoodCabs, I analyzed trip patterns, customer behavior, and operational metrics to provide actionable insights for improving revenue and customer retention in tier-2 Indian markets.

## Key Findings
- **Revenue Leaders**: Jaipur and Lucknow generate 33% of total trips with contrasting pricing strategies
- **Target Performance**: Tourism cities consistently exceed targets while business cities underperform
- **Customer Loyalty**: Business cities show 2-4x higher repeat passenger rates (43% vs 11%)
- **Seasonal Impact**: 60% demand variations during monsoon periods across multiple cities

## Analysis Framework
- **Trip Performance**: Actual vs target analysis across cities and months
- **Customer Segmentation**: New vs repeat passenger behavior patterns  
- **Pricing Strategy**: Fare analysis revealing tourism vs business market dynamics
- **Operational Insights**: Peak/low demand patterns for resource optimization
